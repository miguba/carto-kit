## Customer Support

For order questions, payment confirmation, delivery updates, refund reviews, or product support, email the Joys Box team with your order number and the email address used at checkout.

## What to Include

- Your order number, if you have one.
- The email address used during checkout.
- A short description of the issue or request.
- Screenshots or payment references when they help us verify the problem.

## Billing and Payment Help

If your payment appears pending, duplicated, declined, or charged incorrectly, contact us before opening a dispute so we can check the order and payment logs.

## Refund Requests

Return and refund requests are reviewed under our Return & Refund Policy. We may ask for additional details before approving or declining a request.
