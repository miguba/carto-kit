## Processing Time

Orders are typically processed and dispatched within 1-2 business days after payment confirmation. During peak seasons or promotional events, processing may take up to 3 business days.

## Where We Ship From

Joys Box orders are fulfilled from our warehouse located in the United States. Once your order is processed, it will be shipped directly from our US-based facility to your delivery address.

## Estimated Delivery Time

Once dispatched, most orders arrive within 10-15 business days depending on the destination and shipping method. Delivery times may vary based on local customs procedures, remote areas, or carrier conditions beyond our control.

- Standard Shipping: 10-15 business days after dispatch.
- Delivery timeframes are estimates, not guarantees.
- Orders placed on weekends or public holidays will be processed on the next business day.

## Shipping Rates

Joys Box currently offers free standard shipping on all orders. No additional shipping charges will be applied at checkout.

## Shipping Coverage

We ship to most countries and regions worldwide. If your location is not supported during checkout, contact our support team for assistance.

## Order Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched, you will receive a confirmation email with tracking information when available. You can also contact our support team with your order number for delivery status updates.

## Delivery Issues

If your order has not arrived within the estimated timeframe, or if the package appears damaged upon delivery, contact us as soon as possible.

- For delayed shipments, allow an additional 3-5 business days before reaching out.
- For damaged or missing items, contact our support team with your order number and any relevant photos.
